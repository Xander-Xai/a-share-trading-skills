# Synthetic Research-Universe Screening Example

> `SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE / NOT REAL USER DATA`
>
> Classification: `SYNTHETIC`. All identifiers, counts and states below are invented; they are not derived from any person's watchlist, portfolio, trades or screenshots.

## Purpose

Demonstrate the research process without publishing a user-linked security universe:

```text
synthetic candidate universe
→ deduplicate and validate source coverage
→ screen business evidence and setup quality
→ audit industry/factor concentration
→ retain a research shortlist
→ require fresh Level-0/market/event/risk gates before any action
```

The shortlist is not a portfolio, recommendation or order. The correct output may be no trade.

## Fictional case structure

| Case | Research group | Evidence state | Factor overlap | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CASE-SM-001 | power-equipment theme | public evidence fixture | medium | refresh source and setup gates |
| CASE-SM-002 | materials theme | public evidence fixture | high with CASE-SM-003 | compare within cluster |
| CASE-SM-003 | materials theme | public evidence fixture | high with CASE-SM-002 | avoid counting as independent diversification |

No real instrument, price, date, holding, fill or account value is represented.

## Method lesson

Industry coverage is a completeness diagnostic, not a score bonus or forced holding. Multiple candidates sharing a dominant economic factor are not independent bets. A frozen research candidate never becomes an entry without a fresh score, confirmation, invalidation, executable-risk and authorization review.
